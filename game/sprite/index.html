Walk the character home. 🚶

Five tiny steps. You give the character commands — forward(), turnLeft(), turnRight() — exactly like the move cards in your game. Each page shows an example; you type it into a little JavaScript file, save, reload, and press ▶ Play to watch your character walk. Everyone explores the same open field, walking around ponds, rocks, and bushes to reach the ⭐. Start at 01.

🎮 tip  The character starts in the bottom-left of an open grassy field, and the ⭐ (top-right) is the goal. Type the example into the matching .js file, save with ⌘S, then reload to walk it. ▶ Finished version plays the answer if you're stuck, ⏹ Reset puts the character back at the start — and don't worry, a runaway loop can't freeze the page; the lab stops a walk that goes on too long.
