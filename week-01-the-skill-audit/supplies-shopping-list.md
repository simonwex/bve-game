# Week 1 — The Skill Audit · Supplies Shopping List

For **6 students**, all-paper session. Prices are **estimates in CAD** from Canadian
retailers (Oct 2026); check your own flyers. Most of this is reusable across all 8 weeks,
so it's a one-time buy, not a per-session cost. **You probably already own the ⌂ items.**

---

## Core supplies (needed for Week 1)

| Item | Qty | Why | Where | Est. CAD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | ---: |
| Counting chips / plastic poker chips | ~80 (10 × 6 + spares) | The 10 points each kid allocates on the Stat Sheet | Dollarama / Amazon.ca | $4–8 |
| Index cards (ruled, 3×5) | 1 pack (100+) | The Mechanic Cards | Dollarama / Staples | $2–4 |
| Painter's / masking tape | 1 roll | Taping the playtest grid on a table or floor | ⌂ / Dollarama / Canadian Tire | $3–5 |
| Pencils + erasers | 6+ | Stat Sheets & cards | ⌂ / Dollarama | $2 |
| Fine-tip markers or pens | 1 pack | Writing cards legibly | Dollarama / Staples | $3–5 |
| Small tokens / mini-figures (the "player") | 2–3 | The piece that moves on the grid (LEGO minifig, meeple, or a bottle cap) | ⌂ / Dollarama | $0–4 |
| 6-sided dice (d6) | 2–4 | Optional randomness in a mechanic ("roll to move") | ⌂ / Dollarama | $2–4 |
| Letter-size folders or large envelopes | 8 (6 + 2 spare) | Each kid's Designer's Log | Dollarama / Staples | $3–6 |
| Timer or small bell | 1 | Keeping share-outs to 30 sec | ⌂ (phone works) | $0 |

**Core subtotal: ~$19–38 CAD** (less if the ⌂ items are already on hand)

## The grid — pick ONE

| Option | Notes | Where | Est. CAD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | ---: |
| **A. Taped grid** | Painter's tape on a table/floor, 6×6 or 8×8. Free if you have tape. Reusable. | ⌂ | $0 |
| **B. Grid chart paper** | 1-inch grid flip-chart pad; draw START/GOAL/hazards with marker. More portable. | Staples / Amazon.ca | $18–28 |

Recommended: **Option A** for Week 1 (zero cost, and the physical floor grid is more engaging for this age).

## Print at home (free, just paper + toner)

## Bottom line

A realistic first-time spend is **~$20–40 CAD** at **Dollarama** for almost everything,
plus optional grid paper from **Staples** if you don't want to tape a grid. Nearly all of
it carries forward to the remaining 7 weeks.
