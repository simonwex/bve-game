What's happening: f is the pitch. It starts at 200, and
      each time around we do f = f + 40 to make it bigger, until it reaches 900 — lots
      of tiny beeps climbing. Try + 80 (faster) or + 10 (smoother). For a
      sad-trombone slide down, start high and subtract:
      f = 900; f > 200; f = f - 40.
