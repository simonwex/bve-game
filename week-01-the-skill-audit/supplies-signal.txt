Week 1 game design class - supplies (for 6 kids)
Mostly Dollarama, ~$20-40 CAD total, reusable all 8 weeks.
(check = probably already have it)

CORE
- Counting chips / poker chips, ~80 ($4-8)
- Index cards, ruled 3x5, 1 pack of 100+ ($2-4)
- Painter's or masking tape, 1 roll ($3-5) - check
- Pencils + erasers, 6+ ($2) - check
- Fine-tip markers or pens, 1 pack ($3-5)
- Small tokens / mini-figures for the "player" piece, 2-3
  (LEGO minifig, meeple, or a bottle cap) ($0-4) - check
- 6-sided dice, 2-4 ($2-4) - check
- Letter folders or big envelopes, 8 ($3-6)
- Timer or bell (phone works) - check

GRID - pick ONE
- A) Free: make a 6x6 grid on a table/floor with painter's tape
- B) 1-inch grid chart-paper pad from Staples ($18-28)

PRINT AT HOME
- 6x Player Stat Sheet
- A few blank Mechanic Card templates
  (not made yet)

NICE TO HAVE
- Coloured dot stickers ($2-4)
- Extra red chips/buttons for hazards ($0-3)
- Sticky notes ($2-3)
